title: "Capital Readiness Checklist"
excerpt: "A practical operator-grade checklist for becoming easier to underwrite and easier to approve."
date: "2026-03-14"
---

Most operators do not fail because the business is fake.
They fail because the profile is messy, the documentation is weak, or the use of funds is not framed clearly enough.

Start with:
- entity and documentation cleanup
- revenue clarity
- debt visibility
- stronger positioning
- cleaner capital intent
